Frequently Asked Questions

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measure used to assess body weight relative to height. It's calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI provides a general indication of body weight status but may not account for individual variations in body composition, such as muscle mass, bone density, and fat distribution.

Improving BMI involves adopting a balanced diet, engaging in regular physical activity, getting adequate sleep, and maintaining healthy lifestyle habits. Consult with healthcare professionals for personalized advice.
